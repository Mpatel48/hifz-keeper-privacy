Hifz Keeper Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Hifz Keeper is a Quran revision app made by ATMZ FOREVER LLC. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: there is no account, no ads and no tracking, and what you enter stays on your phone.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your answers to the setup questions (whether you are a hafiz or still memorizing, which Juz you know, how much time you have each day and when), your Daur and revision history, mistake marks and notes, bookmarks, focus sessions, reading position and settings.

All of this is saved only in the app's storage on your phone. We do not have a server that receives it, and we cannot see it.

Recitation audio

The Quran text and fonts are built into the app and work offline. When you play an ayah's recitation, the app downloads that audio file from EveryAyah (everyayah.com). Like any website, their server receives your device's IP address and the name of the file requested. No other information is sent with it. See everyayah.com for how they handle requests.

Reminders and notifications

If you turn on a daily reminder or a focus timer alert, the app asks Android for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

Backups

If you export a backup, the app creates a file with your data and opens Android's share sheet. You choose where it goes (for example a cloud drive or a message to yourself). Android may also include the app's data in your phone's own backup to your Google account if that is turned on in your phone's settings.

What we do not do

No sign-in or account. No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools. We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings > Apps > Hifz Keeper > Storage. Anything you exported as a backup stays wherever you saved it until you delete it.

Children

Hifz Keeper does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
